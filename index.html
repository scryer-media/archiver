Archiver: free archive tool for ZIP, 7z, RAR and TAR

A free archive tool that runs in your browser

Runs on your device

Archives are opened and built in your browser by WebAssembly, compiled code that runs at close to native speed. Nothing is uploaded, there is no account, and it works offline once installed.

Extract any archive

Open ZIP, 7z, RAR (including solid and RAR5), TAR, GZ, XZ, BZ2, Zstandard, Brotli, LZMA and LZ4 files, split archives such as .part1.rar and .001 sets, and password-protected archives.

Create and encrypt

Archiver picks the optimal codec for each file in a 7z archive. Add a password for AES-256 encryption.

Verify and repair

Check and fix damaged downloads with PAR2 and PAR3 recovery files or RAR recovery volumes, and add PAR2 recovery data to any archive you make.

Questions

Is Archiver free?

Yes. Archiver is free to use, with no sign-up and no paid tier.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Archives are opened and created by WebAssembly running in your browser, so your files stay on your device. The page makes no third-party requests.

Why does my browser ask whether to upload files to this site?

Some browsers, such as Chrome, Edge and Brave, show that prompt to any website you let read files from your disk. It only gives Archiver access to read the files so it can build the archive on your device. Your files never leave it: open your browser's developer tools and the Network tab shows no file uploads.

How large a file can it handle?

There is no size limit of its own. Archiver writes to your browser's disk storage, so the limit is your free disk space. Files over 5 GB, including ZIP64 archives, have been tested end to end.

Which archive formats can it open?

ZIP, 7z, RAR (including solid archives and RAR5), TAR, GZ, TGZ, XZ, BZ2, Zstandard, Brotli, LZMA and LZ4, plus split archives such as .part1.rar and .001 sets and password-protected archives.

Can it create RAR files?

No. RAR creation needs WinRAR's proprietary encoder. Archiver creates ZIP, 7z and TAR archives instead, and opens RAR files of any version.

Can it password-protect an archive?

Yes. Adding a password encrypts ZIP and 7z archives with AES-256, and 7z archives can also hide file names.

Can it repair a damaged archive?

Yes, when the archive has recovery data. Archiver verifies and repairs files with PAR2 and PAR3 recovery sets and RAR recovery volumes, and can add PAR2 recovery files to any archive it creates.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. It runs in any modern browser on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iPhone, and can be installed as an app that works offline.
